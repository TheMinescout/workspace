Data & privacy

Conversations are logged to your private Firestore database. You own your data. You can view, manage, and delete all conversation logs at any time through your client portal. I do not share your data with third parties.

The AI infrastructure uses:

  • Cloudflare Workers AI (Llama 3) — processes messages on Cloudflare's infrastructure. Subject to Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.
  • Firebase / Firestore — stores conversation logs, business info, FAQs, and corrections. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

No personally identifiable information is stored beyond what appears in the conversation itself.
