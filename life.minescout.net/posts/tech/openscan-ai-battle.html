OpenScan-AI: One Shot Battle

Posted on 2/18/26 by TheMinescouter

I challenged the world's leading AI models to build OpenScan-AI: a web-based document scanner. The rules: one prompt, one file, and zero human bug fixes during the generation phase. We put Gemini 3.0 Thinking, GPT-5, and Claude 4.6 Extended through the ringer.

While the concept of a browser-based scanner seems simple, handling camera streams and OCR (Optical Character Recognition) in a single-file environment is a high-level architectural challenge. Here is how the battle unfolded.

The Prompt

"Code a complete, single-file HTML5 web application called 'OpenScan-AI' that requires no external assets... Implement a sleek 'Glassmorphism' UI, live camera viewfinder, image processing filters for document clarity, and Tesseract.js integration for actual OCR text extraction."

The Contenders

emoji_events WINNER

Claude

Model: Claude 4.6 Extended

Play Claude's Output »
Minescout's Review:
Claude remains the gold standard for UI/UX in coding tasks. It delivered a stunningly polished interface that worked right out of the box. While all models were "okay" for a first attempt, Claude's version was the most complete, handling the library integrations with the fewest errors.

Google Gemini

Model: Gemini 3.0 Thinking

Play Gemini's Output »
Minescout's Review:
The "Thinking" process led to some very clean JavaScript, but it was light on features. It produced a stable build, but completely forgot to include any method for saving or exporting the scans, making it more of a demo than a tool.

ChatGPT

Model: GPT-5

Play ChatGPT's Output »
Minescout's Review:
GPT-5 went for a very futuristic look, which I appreciated. However, it was "all flash and no go." The actual OCR engine failed to trigger correctly in my tests, making the primary purpose of the app—scanning text—non-functional.

The Verdict

The overall winner is Claude.

Claude's ability to balance technical library requirements with a responsive, high-end UI design allowed it to edge out the competition once again. If you need a "One-Shot" developer, the choice is clear.

🔍 Play the Perfected Version

After the battle, I merged the best design elements of GPT-5 with the robust logic of Claude and Gemini. I added Auto-Scanning, Persistence (LocalStorage), and Manual Image Editing tools to create the ultimate browser-based scanner.
