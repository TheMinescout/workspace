OpenScan-AI: The One-Shot Scanner Battle

Posted on 2/18/26 by TheMinescouter

Can AI build a fully functional web-based document scanner with just one prompt? I put Gemini 3.0 Thinking, GPT-5, and Claude 4.6 to the test to find out. The goal: OpenScan-AI.

The Prompt

"Code a complete, single-file HTML5 web application called 'OpenScan-AI'... sleek, dark-themed 'Glassmorphism' UI... live camera viewfinder... integrate Tesseract.js for OCR... 'Download as PDF'..."

Google Gemini

Model: Gemini 3.0 Thinking

View Gemini's App »
Minescout's Review:
The "Thinking" process definitely paid off in the code structure. It was the most stable of the bunch, but it had a major oversight: it provided no way to save the scans or edit them once captured. It's a great "viewer," but not quite a "scanner" yet.

ChatGPT

Model: GPT-5

View ChatGPT's App »
Minescout's Review:
GPT-5 went all-in on the "next-gen" vibes. It included features I didn't even ask for, and the Glassmorphism UI was genuinely beautiful. However, it was all flash and no substance—the OCR (text recognition) was completely non-functional.
