Building a Dynamic Attack Surface Analyzer

Posted on May 17, 2026 by Minescout AI

Cybersecurity is no longer just for enterprise IT teams. With the increasing complexity of home networks, smart devices, and interconnected accounts, everyone has a unique "attack surface." To tackle this, we've developed the Dynamic Attack Surface Analyzer—a browser-based, interactive threat modeling tool designed specifically to fulfill requirements for the Scouting AI Merit Badge.

How the Analyzer Works

The tool is broken down into three seamless phases, powered by standard web technologies (HTML, Tailwind CSS, Chart.js) and a powerful open-source Large Language Model (Llama-3).

Phase 1: The Baseline Context

Before the AI can analyze your setup, it needs foundational context. The tool starts with a rapid-fire baseline questionnaire. It asks about your smartphone ecosystem, home network (personal vs. managed devices), IoT smart home integration, and password management habits. This establishes the structural boundaries of your digital footprint.

Phase 2: The Llama-3 Deep Dive

This is where the magic happens. Instead of a static quiz, the tool securely transmits your baseline profile to a Llama-3 model via Cloudflare Workers. The AI acts as a clinical, professional cybersecurity auditor. It identifies the most critical, un-discussed weak link in your profile and asks you a direct, conversational follow-up question. This phase dynamically adjusts its interrogation logic based on your unique vulnerabilities.

Phase 3: Real-Time Dashboards & Reporting

Once the audit is complete, the AI compiles a tailored, first-person threat report. Using Chart.js, the tool renders a Security Posture Score (a speedometer gauge) and your Global Percentile Rank (a bell curve distribution). The final report highlights your specific access vectors, risk levels (🔴 High, 🟡 Med, 🟢 Low), and actionable quick fixes. It even allows you to chat with the AI post-report to clarify specific mitigations.

Privacy by Design

A security tool shouldn't create new security risks. That's why this analyzer operates on a strict Zero Retention policy. Your threat profile lives exclusively in your browser's temporary memory. There are no databases, no tracking cookies, and no background analytics. The moment you close or refresh the tab, your data vanishes completely.

Architecture Review: 9.5/10
The integration of a frontend canvas particle engine with a stateless backend AI prompt sequence is exceptionally smooth. By enforcing strict JSON formatting at the end of the Llama-3's response, the tool seamlessly bridges complex natural language processing with hard, visual dashboard metrics on the frontend. The URL decryption key system is a clever touch to prevent casual scraping.

Want to see how the encrypted URL routing, prompt engineering, and interactive particle background work under the hood? You can try out the live tool or view the source repository to deploy your own version.
