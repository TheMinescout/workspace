🔒 Catching Family Thieves with SnackGuard Pro FREE

Posted on 3/25/26 by TheMinescouter

We’ve all been there: you go to the pantry for your favorite treat, only to find the bag suspiciously light. I live with family members who love my snacks a little too much, so I decided to fight back with the latest in AI. Using Claude 4.5 and Cloudflare Workers AI, I built SnackGuard Pro—and it is completely free to use.

This isn't just a simple log; it’s a full-stack security suite for your food. It uses advanced vision models to compare photos and precise weight tracking to ensure nothing goes missing without a record.

The Features

📊 Weight Audits

Log grams using any kitchen scale. The app calculates "Expected Consumption" so it knows if a dip in weight was you or a thief.

👁️ AI Image Diffing

Powered by LLaVA 1.5 via Cloudflare, the AI analyzes bag wrinkles and volume to spot tampering the human eye might miss.

🕵️ Suspect Leaderboard

Track "Theft Incidents" by person. Mark family members with confidence levels and keep a visual gallery of the evidence.

Minescout's Claude 4.5 Review

The Experience:
Building this with the newly released Claude 4.5 was a game-changer. The most impressive part was how it handled the UI design and ease of use. It took the technical complexity of AI vision integration and turned it into a clean, mobile-friendly interface that anyone can set up.

The Verdict:
I actually use this daily to keep my snacks safe from wandering hands. Since it's free and hosts on GitHub Pages, there's no reason not to have it.

Get Started in Minutes

Efficiency was my priority. You can go from "stolen chips" to "active surveillance" in less than 5 minutes:

1. Worker Deploy: One command to Cloudflare launches your AI "brain" for free.
2. Launch the App: Access the app instantly via GitHub Pages.
3. Protect: Enter your snack's starting weight and you're officially guarded.

SnackGuard Pro is open-source, free, and live right now. Stop the snack theft today.
