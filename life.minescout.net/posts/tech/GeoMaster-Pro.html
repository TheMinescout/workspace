GeoMaster Pro: The Ultimate Custom Map Quizzer

Posted on 4/7/26 by TheMinescouter

Have you ever wanted to study a specific region of the globe, but found that standard geography apps only offer pre-packaged quizzes? That frustration is exactly what led to the creation of GeoMaster Pro.

Instead of being locked into "World Capitals" or "US States", GeoMaster Pro lets you paste in absolutely any list of locations—cities, countries, or regions—and instantly generates an interactive, sleek, map-based quiz right in your browser.

The Tech Stack

Built entirely as a single-file HTML application, GeoMaster Pro leverages Tailwind CSS for its modern UI, Leaflet.js for interactive map rendering, and the OpenStreetMap Nominatim API to dynamically fetch and draw geographical boundaries on the fly.

Core Features

Dynamic Play Modes

GeoMaster Pro comes with two distinct ways to interact with your data:

  • Quiz Mode: A timed, scored challenge where you must click the correct location on a blank map. You get three strikes per location, with dynamic coloring based on your performance.
  • Learn Mode: A guided tour of your custom list. The map automatically pans and highlights each target area, helping you build spatial memory before you take the quiz.

Intelligent UI & Scoring

The app features a "Performance Bar" that tracks your session accuracy in real-time, assigning you a final grade (S, A, B, C, or F) upon completion. It also includes local browser caching, meaning if you query the same locations tomorrow, the map will load instantly without waiting on the API.

Minescout's Official Review:
"GeoMaster Pro is incredibly responsive. The way the map seamlessly zooms to fit whatever custom bounding boxes the API returns feels like magic. The addition of the 'Auto-Advance' toggle and the visual hint system (where the map slightly zooms toward the target) are massive quality-of-life improvements. The only downside is relying on the free OpenStreetMap API, which can occasionally be slow if you input a massive list of 50+ obscure towns all at once."

Developer Interview

We sat down with TheMinescouter to talk about the development process behind the tool.

Q: What was the primary inspiration behind GeoMaster Pro?
A: I originally needed a way to practice for my history tests. There are plenty of geography games out there, but I specifically wanted the ability to fully customize the locations I was being tested on, rather than being stuck with pre-made lists.
Q: What was the biggest technical hurdle you faced while building it?
A: The biggest challenge was calculating the map zoom and framing. I had to take the bounding box data from the OpenStreetMap API, convert it into Leaflet coordinate bounds, and apply a visual buffer (padding) so the clickable GeoJSON polygons wouldn't be cut off at the edges of the screen. Getting the camera to smoothly track the collection of polygons took some trial and error.
Q: Do you have any exciting plans for future updates or new features?
A: Coming very soon, I'm hoping to add built-in presets for every single continent, making it even easier to jump right into a game without having to type out your own lists!
