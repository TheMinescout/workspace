Minescouts Eagle Progress: The Home Stretch

Originally posted on April 11, 2025 by The Minescout Scouter | Updated February 17, 2026

The journey to Eagle Scout is nearly complete. After years of camping, merit badges, and service, I am officially in the final stage. Below is the updated progress toward the highest rank in BSA Scouting.

Rank Progress
96%

Eagle Project: Little Free Library

Completed & Installed

The Little Free Library was successfully installed as planned in September 2025. It stands as a permanent fixture in the community, providing free books for all neighbors.

The Numbers & Logistics

The project totaled 69 hours of service. While the physical work was rewarding, we faced a real-world hurdle when a wrong kit was ordered that couldn't be returned, putting the project slightly over budget—a valuable lesson in logistics.

The Leadership Lesson:
"The hardest part of this project wasn't the construction; it was learning to step back. As a leader, I had to resist the urge to do everything myself and trust my team to help. Letting go of total control allowed the project to move faster and gave others a chance to contribute."

Merit Badges & Paperwork

I have officially completed all merit badges required for the Eagle rank. All five Letters of Recommendation from my teachers have been received and accepted by the council. The formal Eagle Scout Rank Application has been submitted and verified.

🗓️ Final Step: Board of Review

Everything has led to this. My Eagle Board of Review (BOR) is officially scheduled for March 11, 2026. This is the final interview where I represent my journey and my project to the district committee.

The End

Thanks for following this journey.
The Minescout Scouters.
