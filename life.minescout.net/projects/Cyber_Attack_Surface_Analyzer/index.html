AI Ethics & Data Transparency

Before the AI Analyzes Your Setup...

1. Data Privacy (Zero Retention)

This tool collects absolutely no data. Your threat profile lives exclusively in your browser's temporary memory. Once you close this tab, your data vanishes. There are no databases, tracking cookies, or analytics.

2. Ethical Objective (White Hat AI)

This AI is strictly programmed for defensive threat modeling. It analyzes vulnerabilities to help you secure your digital footprint, adhering to ethical cybersecurity practices. It will not execute code, test your network, or ask for sensitive passwords.

3. Architecture & Accessibility

Individually coded for the Scouting AI Merit Badge, this project leverages completely free, self-sufficient infrastructure. It interfaces with an open-source Large Language Model (Llama-3) via Cloudflare Workers to provide advanced analysis at zero cost.
